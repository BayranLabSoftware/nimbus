# FCM round — register of deviations

Rule 1144 (`src/physics/validation/fcmRoundRules.ts`) asks round 1's package for a register of
deviations with their reasons: every place where the branch, its verification or its reading departs
from the dossier as written (rules 1137 to 1147), or where the dossier left a choice open that the
work had to close. Entries are added as they arise and are never removed; an entry the reviewer
rejects is marked so, with the change it caused. Everything here is development, begun before the
reviewer has read the dossier (rule 1147).
