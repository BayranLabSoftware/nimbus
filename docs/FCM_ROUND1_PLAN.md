# FCM round 1 — the plan, on the reviewer's leave

Rules 1148 to 1159 (`src/physics/validation/fcmRound1Rules.ts`), written and pushed before any
development run. The opening dossier is rules 1137 to 1147 (`docs/FCM_ROUND_DOSSIER.md`).

## The leave (rule 1148)

Round 1 runs whole on the declared development cases, with no further approval before the
development runs, and ends in one package. Its proposal is «ready for the independent test», «ready
in a restricted domain» or «not ready» — never an adoption. Gates 1 and 2 are not declared passed on
the summary already sent: the package shows the exceptions, the draws not completed and the
discrepancies with W18. A change to an observable's definition, a gate's criterion or the access to
the reserved set is a revision of the plan, written as a rule before it is used.

Round 2: the reconnaissance of candidates only, after round 1's tuning is frozen, from titles,
abstracts and metadata, with no download (Andrea). The fourth set's constitution and the charter v3
come later, in one final preparation dossier.
